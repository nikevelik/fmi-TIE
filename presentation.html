Who we serve

Customer Segments

🚗
Urban Drivers

Commuters who regularly need parking in busy city centers. Want fast, reliable info on available spots to reduce daily stress.

🗺️
Visitors & Occasional Drivers

Tourists, shoppers, or event-goers unfamiliar with local parking patterns. Need easy guidance to free spaces in real time.

🏛️
Municipalities

City administrations interested in reducing congestion, optimizing parking infrastructure, and monitoring urban mobility.

What we offer

Value Propositions

📍
Real-Time Availability

Shows which parking spots are free right now, powered by live drone feeds.

🔮
Predictive Guidance

Historical data estimates where spots are likely to open soon before you even arrive.

⏱️
Time & Stress Savings

Dramatically cuts time spent circling blocks looking for parking.

🌿
Traffic & Fuel Efficiency

Reduces unnecessary driving, lowering congestion and emissions across the city.

📱
Seamless Mobile Access

Available through a mobile app for convenience, anytime and anywhere.

📊
Operational Insights

Parking operators and municipalities can monitor usage and optimize space management.

🛡️ Trust & Reliability

How we operate

Key Activities & Resources

Key Activities
🚁 Drone Operations

Patrolling urban areas, keeping drones functional and flight-compliant.

👁️ Data Capture & Processing

Collecting aerial images and running computer vision algorithms.

🗺️ Real-Time Map Updates

Continuously integrating drone data into the app and predictive model.

📈 Predictive Analytics

Refining historical data to forecast parking availability.

Key Resources
🚁 Autonomous Drone Fleet

Hardware capable of safe urban flight and precision image capture.

🤖 Computer Vision Software

Algorithms to detect parking occupancy accurately from aerial imagery.

☁️ Data Infrastructure

Cloud storage, real-time processing, and mapping platforms.

📜 Regulatory Permits

Legal clearance for urban drone operations from relevant authorities.

Who we work with

🏭
Drone Manufacturers

🤖
AI / CV Tech Providers

Telecom & Cloud Providers

Mapping Platforms

🅿️
Parking Operators

Municipal and private lot operators who grant access and benefit from usage optimization insights.

Business model economics

Revenue Streams & Cost Structure

Revenue Streams
💳 Subscriptions

Drivers pay monthly/yearly for real-time access and predictive analytics.

🎟️ Pay-Per-Use

Occasional users pay per session — no commitment needed.

🏢 B2B Partnerships

Parking operators, municipalities, and logistics companies pay for data integration.

📣 Advertising

Local businesses sponsor highlighted zones or promoted areas in the app.

Cost Structure
🚁 Drone Fleet

Purchase, maintenance, batteries, and replacement cycles.

💻 Software & AI

Development, updates, cloud computing, and computer vision processing.

👥 Personnel

Engineers, drone operators, and customer support staff.

📋 Regulatory & Insurance

Drone flight permits, liability coverage, and compliance fees.
